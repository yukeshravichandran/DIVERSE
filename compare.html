Diverse modules

Diverse modules are intricately connected, each serving specific functions essential for streamlined HR management.

Attendence

Effortlessly manage attendance with precise tracking and insightful reporting features. The attendance module ensures seamless monitoring of employee attendance.

Recruitment

Effectively oversee job postings, interviews, and candidate advancement with our streamlined recruitment capabilities. Recruitment module simplifies sourcing and hiring processes seamlessly.

Onboarding

Experience a revolutionary approach to employee onboarding with Diverse's advanced, intuitive platform. Simplifying the induction process for new employees seamlessly.

Employees

Effortlessly handle employee data and requests with our comprehensive management software.

Assets

Efficiently oversee company asset allocations and employee requests with our Asset Management Software.

PMS

Performance Management System module facilitates employee performance assessment and feedback, integrating OKRs and 360-degree evaluations for effective management.

Payroll

The payroll module in Diverse automates accurate payroll processing, efficiently managing employee compensation, tax calculations, and payslip generation with our open-source software.

Leave

Enhance leave management efficiency through streamlined tracking and request processing for employees.
